The Philosophy

Why $KIBBLE Matters

Most memecoins exist solely for temporary excitement. At $KIBBLE, we believe meme power can be channeled into an unstoppable force for good without losing the humor and hype that makes crypto fun.

Around the world, over 200 million stray animals fight hunger and severe conditions daily. By establishing a passionate community and leveraging creator-driven funding mechanics, we prove that Web3 culture can fill real food bowls on the ground.

Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Is $KIBBLE a registered charity?

No. $KIBBLE is a decentralized, community-driven cryptocurrency token. It does not solicit or collect charitable donations directly. All animal welfare contributions, kibble distribution, and shelter sponsorships are funded and executed voluntarily by the developer team from protocol allocations.

How do I verify the feeding proofs?

Every animal feeding run, kibble purchase, and shelter aid operation will be recorded and shared publicly on our official Telegram and X accounts with date timestamps and clear documentation.

When will the token launch?

We are currently in the pre-launch phase, building an organic, strong, and dedicated community. The exact launch date and contract address will be announced well in advance exclusively on our official channels.
