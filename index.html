Closed-Loop Ecosystem

Volume ➔ Kibble ➔ Proof Feedback Loop

🥣

$KIBBLE operates on a sustainable closed-loop mechanism where on-chain volume directly powers tangible feeding initiatives and sends verifiable proof back to the community.

01 • Volume & Protocol Fees

Decentralized trading activity generates developer proceeds. Zero public donation campaigns.

02 • Wholesale Kibble & Shelter Support

Proceeds fund wholesale dry pet food purchases, street feeding routes, and direct shelter aid.

03 • On-Chain Verification

Disbursements and operations are verifiable directly on Solana blockchain explorers.
