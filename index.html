❓ Clarifications & Protocol Mechanics

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Kibble, smart contract security, shelter allocations, and founder commitments.

01 What is Kibble?
Kibble ($KIBBLE) is an animal welfare-oriented Web3 project built on the Solana blockchain that converts viral meme culture and decentralized trading activity into tangible food aid for stray animals and underfunded shelters. Unlike typical speculative memecoins without real-world utility, Kibble channels on-chain volume into verifiable, physical relief.
02 How do donations reach animal shelters?
We never conduct manual crowdfunding or solicit public donations. Using the native Solana Token-2022 transfer fee mechanism, a deterministic 2% fee is harvested from every on-chain transfer directly into the protocol shelter treasury. These funds are periodically deployed for wholesale dry kibble purchases. Procurement invoices, distribution routes, and shelter deliveries are publicly documented through video and photo logs ("Proof-of-Feed") alongside on-chain transaction hashes.
02 Is the Kibble smart contract secure?

Yes. The contract is deployed under immutable and decentralized Web3 architecture:

  • Solana Token-2022 Standard: Employs Solana's native, battle-tested extensions without third-party proxy risks.
  • Mint Authority Revoked: Token creation privileges are permanently burned. Total supply is mathematically capped at 10,000,000 tokens and can never increase.
  • Freeze Authority Revoked: No administrative entity can freeze holder assets or maintain wallet blacklists.
  • 100% LP Burned: Initial liquidity pool tokens are transferred to a dead address at launch, eliminating the possibility of liquidity pull (rug-pull) exploits.
04 Why invest in Kibble?

Kibble differentiates itself through three foundational pillars:

  • Real-World Utility: Volume naturally turns into food aid for stray animals.
  • 100% Fair Launch: Zero private presales, zero venture capital allocations, and zero team supply allocations—eliminating insider dumping risks.
  • Sustainable Community Moat: Merging philanthropic impact with high-velocity Solana culture creates durable community loyalty.
05 Why trust the founder and team?
In Web3, trust should be placed in code, not promises. The Kibble architecture eliminates discretionary admin control: the developer cannot mint new tokens, modify fee rates, or remove liquidity pool capital because all administrative keys are permanently revoked on-chain. Additionally, with zero creator token allocations, there is no treasury overhang to dump on the open market.
06 How does the system operate?
The protocol operates through a three-stage closed-loop feedback mechanism:
  1. Trading Volume: Every decentralized exchange swap or peer-to-peer transfer incurs a 2% native protocol fee.
  2. Treasury Accumulation: Fees route deterministically to the dedicated animal shelter reserve wallet without manual intermediaries.
  3. Food Conversion & Verification: Reserves are converted to bulk animal food and delivered to partner shelters, followed by video and photo proofs published with on-chain verification data.
