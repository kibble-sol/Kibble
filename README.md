Every click on the screen feeds a stray animal in the real world.

🌟 Overview

Kibble is an impact-first tap-to-feed dApp built on Solana. Users tap to feed stray animals, while transactions and engagement generate protocol-driven funding via Token-2022 transfer fees to buy and distribute physical food on the streets.

🌐 Live Web dApp: kibble-sol.github.io/Kibble/

✈️ Telegram Hub: @kibblesol

🐦 X (Twitter): @kibblesol

⚙️ Core Architecture & Mechanics

Tap-to-Feed Engine: Simple, lightweight web interface. Users tap to generate feeding points that convert into real-world food supplies.

Token-2022 Transfer Fee: Native transfer fee extension automatically collects protocol revenue from transactions and sends it directly to the animal feeding treasury.

Proof of Feed (Real Impact): Accumulated funds are converted into physical kibble bags, distributed to street animals, and verified on-chain and via video documentation.
